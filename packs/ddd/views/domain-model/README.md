# The domain model

The DDD pattern's page in the product's studio (`place: design`, after the architecture): the areas of
the business and how important each is, the parts of the model and who owns them, how they work
together, what people do with them, what they are told to do and what happens, the core business
objects and their rules, and what people consult. Written as sentences in the business's words, from
the knowledge base; the domain model's open questions (`DD`) are listed at the bottom.
